---
title: Your first day
summary: Wood, a table, a pickaxe, a torch, a furnace — in that order, and why that order.
---

Everything in SynthMiner needs something else first, and the first hour is
mostly working up that chain. Here is the short version. Each step links to the
page that explains it properly.

## 1. Punch a tree

Face a tree trunk and hold <kbd>Q</kbd>. **The whole tree comes down at once** —
trunk, branches and leaves — and every log of it drops. You also get **one or two
saplings**, which is how you get wood back later; put them in the ground when you
have somewhere to put them. See [Trees and saplings](plants/trees.html).

Six or eight logs is plenty to start.

## 2. Make planks, sticks and a crafting table

Press <kbd>C</kbd> for the crafting book. There is no grid to arrange — you pick
what you want to make from a list and it tells you what it needs. Four things can
be made with bare hands:

| Make | From |
|---|---|
| 4 planks | 1 log |
| 4 sticks | 2 planks |
| 4 torches | 1 coal + 1 stick |
| A crafting table | 4 planks |

Put the table down with <kbd>E</kbd>. Everything from here on wants you to be
standing at one.

<p class="note"><b>The book crafts the intermediate steps for you.</b> Ask for a
wooden pickaxe while holding nothing but logs and it will make the planks and the
sticks on the way. If anything in the chain is missing it makes nothing at all and
gives your materials back untouched.</p>

## 3. A wooden pickaxe, then a stone one

At the table: **3 planks + 2 sticks** is a wooden pickaxe. Wood only lasts 60
blocks, and its one real job is to get you stone — stone will not drop
cobblestone for a fist, however long you hit it.

Dig up eight or ten cobblestone, then make a **stone pickaxe** (3 cobblestone + 2
sticks) and a **stone axe**. Stone is twice as fast as wood and lasts 130 blocks,
and only a stone pickaxe or better will open **iron ore**.

Also worth making while you are there: an **axe** for wood and a **shovel** for
dirt, sand and gravel. See [Tools](tools/index.html).

## 4. A furnace, and light

**8 cobblestone** is a [furnace](devices/furnace.html). It smelts one thing at a
time and burns anything wooden as fuel — logs, planks, sticks, broken wooden
tools — or coal, which lasts far longer.

The furnace is also where you get coal if you have not found any: **1 log in →
1 coal out**. Coal plus a stick is four [torches](devices/torch.html), and a
torch is the difference between a cave you can dig and a hole you fall down.

## 5. Find coal and iron

Both are in veins, not single blocks — find one and dig sideways.

- **Coal** appears anywhere below **y = 40**, which is most of the way up a
  hillside. Plentiful.
- **Iron** only below **y = 28**, and you need a stone pickaxe for it.

Press <kbd>Backspace</kbd> to see what height you are at.
See [Ores and stone](ores/index.html).

Smelt the ore in the furnace to get **iron ingots**. Iron makes tools that last
250 blocks, and three other things that matter more than the tools do: a
[bucket](tools/bucket.html), [shears](tools/shears.html), and eventually a
[kitchen stove](devices/kitchen-stove.html).

## 6. Somewhere to put things, and somewhere to wake up

- A **[chest](devices/chest.html)** is 8 planks, and holds 27 stacks.
- A **[bed](devices/bed.html)** is 3 wool + 3 planks. Wool wants shears and
  shears want iron, so the bed usually arrives on day two — but it is the single
  most useful thing in the game: it skips the night and it moves where you
  respawn.

## 7. Eat something

You start full and you will not notice hunger for a while, but it does go down.

The quickest food on day one is a **tomato** — the only thing in the game you can
eat raw off the plant — or **milk**, if you have a bucket and have found a cow.
Everything else is an ingredient until a stove has been at it.

Then: a [hoe](tools/hoe.html) and a field, a [composter](devices/composter.html)
for the weeds, and a [kitchen stove](devices/kitchen-stove.html) that turns a
chestful of farm produce into real meals. That is the second day, and it is where
most of the game is.

See [Hunger and saturation](mechanics/hunger.html) and
[Plants and farming](plants/index.html).

## What kills you

Not much, yet. There are no monsters in this version. What there is:

- **Falling.** Three blocks are free; after that it is half a heart a block,
  and a long drop is fatal. Water at the bottom cancels all of it.
- **Starving.** An empty hunger bar takes health, slowly but without stopping.

Dying costs you **nothing but half your health** — you keep your whole
inventory and wake up at your bed or your spawn. See
[Health, falling and death](mechanics/health.html).
