## Shearing

Hold **shears** and press <kbd>E</kbd> on an adult sheep. You get **1 to 3 wool**,
the shears wear by one, and the sheep is left shorn.

A **shorn sheep gives nothing** until its fleece has grown back, which takes **twenty
minutes of playing**. You can see which is which: a shorn sheep is drawn differently, so you
are not walking round a flock trying every one of them.

That twenty minutes is twenty minutes of *your* time, and it only counts while the sheep is
in a loaded chunk near you. Unlike a composter or a cheese barrel, **a fleece does not grow
back while you sleep**, and it does not grow in a field you have walked away from.

Lambs cannot be sheared.

<p class="note"><b>This is the only thing an animal gives more than once</b> —
apart from a cow's milk. Everything else a creature gives, it gives when it dies.
A flock of sheep is a renewable resource; a flock of pigs is a larder.</p>

## Why wool matters

| Use | Needs |
|---|---|
| [**Bed**](../devices/bed.html) | **3 wool** + 3 planks |
| **String** | 1 wool → **3 string** at a crafting table |
| [**Fishing rod**](../tools/fishing-rod.html) | 3 sticks + **2 string** |
| **Compost** | Wool is the one animal product that rots down |

The bed is the important one. It skips the night and it sets where you wake up when
you die, and it is the only thing in the game that wants wool.

After that: **string**. One fleece is three string, and a fishing rod wants two —
so a rod costs about two thirds of one sheep's shearing. That is what makes
[fishing](../mechanics/fishing.html) a thing you do once you have a flock rather
than on your first afternoon.

String does not go back into wool.

## Mutton

Killing a sheep gives 1–2 mutton, which is fewer than a pig or a cow gives. Mutton
is not food raw, and it makes the two best meat dishes:

| Use | Needs |
|---|---|
| **Mutton mash** — 5 hunger, 2 saturation | 1 mutton + **2 potatoes** |
| **Kebab** — **8 hunger, 7 saturation** | 1 mutton + 1 bean + 2 wheat + 1 tomato |

The kebab is the second-best food in the game, beaten only by the pizza, and it is
the best thing you can make without a fishing rod.

## Keeping a flock

Sheep eat **wheat**, like cows, so one wheat field feeds both. Two sheep in a pen,
sheared every day, is enough wool for a bed, a rod, and a steady trickle of compost
from the surplus.

Mountains are the other place to look for them, and the only animal that lives up
there.
