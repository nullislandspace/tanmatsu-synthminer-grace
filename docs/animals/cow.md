## Milk

Hold an empty [**bucket**](../tools/bucket.html) and press <kbd>E</kbd> on an adult
cow. The bucket becomes a bucket of milk. There is no cooldown and no limit — a
cow is an infinite supply of milk for the price of walking up to it.

A calf gives nothing; it says so rather than doing nothing.

Milk is worth having twice over:

- **You can drink it.** It is one of only two things in the game you can eat
  without cooking (the other is a raw tomato), and the **pail comes back empty**
  when you do.
- **It makes cheese.** One bucket of milk in a [cheese maker](../devices/cheese-maker.html)
  becomes one cheese, and the bucket comes straight back the moment the milk goes
  in — it is not locked up for the day the cheese takes.

Cheese is worth 2 hunger and 2 saturation on its own, and it is **one of the four
things a pizza needs.**

<p class="note"><b>One cow and one bucket is a food supply.</b> Before you have a
farm at all, a cow you have penned in is drinkable food on demand. It is the
cheapest answer to hunger in the early game.</p>

## Feeding and breeding

A cow eats **wheat**, which is the crop you can have on day one — see
[Wheat](../plants/wheat.html). That makes cows the easiest animal to breed, and
worth doing: two cows in a pen with a wheat field beside them is as close to solved
as food gets.

Sheep eat wheat too, so one field feeds both.

## Beef

Not food until it is cooked.

| Use | Needs |
|---|---|
| **Steak and potatoes** — 5 hunger, 2 saturation | 1 beef + 1 potato, on a [kitchen stove](../devices/kitchen-stove.html) |
| **Feeding a dog** | 1 beef |

Beef is also what a tamed [dog](dog.html) eats, which is the only reason to keep
any of it raw.
