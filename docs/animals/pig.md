## Finding one

Pigs are the most widespread animal — they live in three of the five biomes, and
forests are as good a bet as plains. They are slow, which makes them the easiest to
herd and the easiest to catch.

## Feeding

A pig takes **either a potato or beans**, which is deliberately awkward: both of
those are crops *you* want. That is the choice — a pig costs you food to make more
food.

Beans are usually the better answer. They are easier to find than potatoes, they
have no raw form so you are not eating them anyway, and two beans make a
vegetarian sausage whereas a potato makes a fairly poor baked potato.

See [Animals](index.html#feeding-and-breeding) for how breeding works.

## What a pig is worth

Pork is not food until something cooks it.

| Use | Needs |
|---|---|
| **Pork and beans** — 5 hunger, 2 saturation | 1 pork + 1 bean, on a [kitchen stove](../devices/kitchen-stove.html) |
| **Sausage** — 2 hunger, 2 saturation | 1 pork + **1 flower** (either colour), in a [sausage maker](../devices/sausage-maker.html) |

## The bone

This is the real reason to keep pigs.

Every pork sausage the [sausage maker](../devices/sausage-maker.html) turns out has
roughly a **1 in 6** chance of leaving a **bone** in its second output slot. A bone
is the only thing that [tames a dog](dog.html), and there is no other source of one
anywhere in the game.

A bean sausage never leaves a bone — the bone comes off the meat. So:

> **dog = pig + flower + patience.**

Six or seven sausages is usually a bone. Keep the sausage maker running.
