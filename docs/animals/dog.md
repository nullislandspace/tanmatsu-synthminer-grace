## Finding one

Dogs are the rare creature, and the only one you go looking for on purpose. A
**forest** is the best place: about one forest chunk in twelve has one or two. Birch
woods are nearly as good. There is a thin scatter over the plains — roughly one
chunk in a hundred — which is where most people meet their first one, because the
plains are four fifths of the world.

They are fast and they wander, so a dog you have seen and not tamed may be gone
when you come back with a bone.

## Getting a bone

There is **exactly one** source of bones in the game:

> A pork **sausage** leaves a bone behind roughly **one time in six**.

So a dog costs a [pig](pig.html), a flower, a
[sausage maker](../devices/sausage-maker.html) (nine iron ingots), and some
patience. A vegetarian sausage never leaves one — the bone comes off the meat.

The bone appears in the sausage maker's **second output slot**, which is what that
slot is for.

## Taming

Hold the bone and press <kbd>E</kbd> on the dog. That is all — one bone, one dog,
no repeated attempts and no chance of failure.

## Living with one

| | |
|---|---|
| **It follows you** whenever you are more than 4 blocks away, and it is faster than you, so it keeps up |
| **Press <kbd>E</kbd> on it** (with anything that is not beef) to make it **sit** — and again to make it stand. This is how you leave a dog somewhere on purpose instead of taking it down a mine |
| **Left too far behind** — more than 18 blocks — it gives up walking and **appears beside you**. A dog cannot be lost to a cliff or a lake |
| **It eats beef**, which puts it in the mood to breed like any other animal. Two tamed dogs will give you a puppy |

## It is not livestock

A dog drops nothing when it dies. No meat, no bone, nothing. Killing one pays
exactly zero, which is deliberate.

What you get instead is something that follows you around and is quite hard to lose.
