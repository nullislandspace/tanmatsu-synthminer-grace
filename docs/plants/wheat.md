## Getting started

Wheat is the crop you can have on your first day, because you do not have to find
it — you have to walk through some grass. **Tall grass** grows all over the plains
and forests, and breaking it drops a wheat seed about half the time.

Collect a dozen seeds, till a patch beside water, and plant the lot. In twenty
minutes you have thirty-odd wheat and enough seed for a bigger field.

## What it is for

| Use | Needs |
|---|---|
| **Bread** — 2 hunger, 1 saturation | 3 wheat, on a [kitchen stove](../devices/kitchen-stove.html) |
| **Kebab** — 8 hunger, 7 saturation | 2 wheat + mutton + beans + tomato |
| **Pizza** — 10 hunger, 8 saturation | 2 wheat + sausage + cheese + 2 shrimp |
| **Feeding a cow** | 1 wheat |
| **Feeding a sheep** | 1 wheat |

Wheat is the single most useful crop in the game because it does two jobs: it is in
the two best dishes, and it is what both a [cow](../animals/cow.html) and a
[sheep](../animals/sheep.html) eat. A cow fed wheat breeds; a calf follows whoever
is holding it. You will want more wheat than you first think.

## Bread is not very good

Worth saying plainly: three wheat for 2 hunger is a poor deal, and bread is the
weakest thing the stove makes. Wheat's value is as an **ingredient** in the pizza
and the kebab, and as **animal feed**. Bake bread when you have nothing else; grow
wheat because everything else wants it.
