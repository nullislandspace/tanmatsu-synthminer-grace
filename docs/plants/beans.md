| | |

| **Seed** | A bean — it plants itself |
| **Grows on** | Wet farmland |
| **Seed to ripe** | **2 in-game days** — 40 minutes of playing |
| **Yield** | 1–3 beans per plant |
| **Composts** | Yes |

## Finding the first one

Wild bean plants grow **in forests**, about three times as often as a wild potato
turns up in the plains. Forests are uncommon but not rare, so beans are the
easiest of the three land crops to find — go and look in the trees.

## What it is for

Beans do more different jobs than any other crop.

| Use | Needs |
|---|---|
| **Baked beans** — 2 hunger | 1 bean, on a [kitchen stove](../devices/kitchen-stove.html) |
| **Pork and beans** — 5 hunger, 2 saturation | 1 bean + pork |
| **Kebab** — 8 hunger, 7 saturation | 1 bean + mutton + 2 wheat + tomato |
| **Vegetarian sausage** — 2 hunger, 2 saturation | **2 beans**, in a [sausage maker](../devices/sausage-maker.html) |
| **Feeding a pig** | 1 bean (or a potato) |

<p class="note"><b>One bean for 2 hunger is the floor the whole food table stands
on.</b> Beans have no raw form at all — you cannot eat one — so baked beans are the
cheapest cooked meal in the game, and everything else is priced against them.</p>

## The vegetarian sausage

Two beans in a sausage maker make a **fake sausage**, worth exactly what a pork
sausage is worth: 2 hunger and 2 saturation. And it **counts as a pizza's sausage**
— the pizza will accept either kind.

That matters more than it sounds. A pizza is the best food in the game (10 hunger,
8 saturation) and the sausage is normally the part that needs a pig. With beans you
can make one without killing anything.

The pizza still wants two shrimp, so it is not actually a vegetarian dish. But the
sausage in it can be.

## A bean sausage leaves no bone

The pork sausage occasionally drops a **bone** as a second output, and a bone is
what [tames a dog](../animals/dog.html). A bean sausage never does — the bone
comes off the meat. If you want a dog, you need a pig.
