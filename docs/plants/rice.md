## Rice does not use farmland

This is the one crop a hoe has nothing to do with. Rice is planted **into the
water cell itself**, and the conditions are exact:

- the water must be **one block deep** — one cell of water with air above it, not
  two;
- it must be standing on **sand**, not dirt or stone;
- it must be a **source**, not flowing water passing through.

Point at the *water* and press <kbd>E</kbd>. If any of the three is wrong the game
says the ground is not right rather than taking the seed.

## Finding a place for it

Two options:

**The natural way.** Every shore in the world is sand, whatever biome is behind it,
so the shallows of any lake or sea are a rice paddy waiting to happen. Wade out
until the water is over your feet but not over your head, look down — you can see
through water — and find the strip where it is exactly one deep.

**The built way.** Dig a one-deep trench, floor it with **sand**, and fill it with
a bucket. Two water sources in a trench make a spring, so you can flood as long a
paddy as you like from one bucket. This is much easier than hunting for the right
depth, and it lets you put the paddy next to the rest of your farm.

## Finding the first rice

Wild rice grows in exactly the conditions above, on sandy shores. It is not common
but it is not hidden either — walk a shoreline and look at the shallows. It is
generated ripe.

## What it is for

| Use | Needs |
|---|---|
| **Rice patty** — 3 hunger, 1 saturation | **2 rice**, on a [kitchen stove](../devices/kitchen-stove.html) |
| **Sashimi** — 4 hunger | 1 rice + 1 salmon |

Sashimi is the best thing rice does and the reason to keep a paddy: 4 hunger from
one fish and one grain, with no meat and no other crop involved. If you are fishing
anyway, rice doubles what the fish is worth.
