| **Seed** | A potato — it plants itself, no crafting needed |
| **Grows on** | Wet farmland |
| **Seed to ripe** | **2 in-game days** — 40 minutes of playing |
| **Yield** | 1–3 potatoes per plant |
| **Composts** | Yes |

## Finding the first one

This is the awkward part. Wild potato plants grow **in the plains**, at about one
column in seven thousand — one or two somewhere in a plain you can see across.
They are generated ripe, so the one you find is also your first harvest, and a
single find gives you 1–3 potatoes to plant.

There is no other way to get one. Keep looking at the ground while you walk.

<p class="note"><b>Never eat your last potato.</b> A potato is a seed, and a
baked potato is only worth 1 hunger. Until you have a field of them, a potato in
your hand is worth more in the ground.</p>

## What it is for

| Use | Needs |
|---|---|
| **Baked potato** — 1 hunger, 1 saturation | 1 potato, on a [kitchen stove](../devices/kitchen-stove.html) |
| **Steak and potatoes** — 5 hunger, 2 saturation | 1 potato + beef |
| **Mutton mash** — 5 hunger, 2 saturation | **2 potatoes** + mutton |
| **Feeding a pig** | 1 potato (or beans) |

The potato's real value is in the two meat dishes, which are among the best food in
the game for what goes into them. Its second value is **pigs**: a potato in your
hand makes a piglet follow you, and feeding two adult pigs breeds them.

Two days is a long time for one plant, so plant a lot of them at once.
