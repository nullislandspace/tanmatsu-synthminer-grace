## Yellow and red flowers

Scattered over grassland. Both break instantly, drop themselves, and rot down in a
composter.

They look like decoration and they are not: **a sausage needs one flower** —
either colour — along with the pork. A farm that makes sausages eats flowers
steadily, which is why one of them had to be renewable.

### Growing yellow flowers

**Throw compost on the ground.** Point at the top of a **grass** block with
[compost](../devices/composter.html) in hand and press <kbd>E</kbd>, and a **3×3
patch of yellow flowers** grows around that cell.

It skips any cell in the nine that is not clear grass, and it only refuses outright
if none of them would take — so a patch against a wall still works, it just gives
you fewer.

This is the only way to *make* flowers, and it is how a sausage farm stops being
limited by how much grassland you have walked over.

<p class="note"><b>Red flowers do not grow back.</b> There is no way to make one —
they are found and that is all. It does not block anything: the sausage maker takes
either colour, so yellow alone is enough for everything in the game.</p>

## Tall grass

Also scattered over grassland and forest, also instant to break. It drops a
**wheat seed** about half the time, and **that is where wheat comes from** — there
is no other source of the first seeds. See [Wheat](wheat.html).

Tall grass composts too.

## Cactus

Grows only in the **sand flats**, in columns one to three blocks tall. Breaks
easily, needs no tool, and drops itself.

Breaking any part of a cactus **takes the whole column down at once**, the way a
tree does.

There is nothing to do with a cactus yet. It does not compost, it is not food, and
there is no recipe for it. It is scenery you can carry, and a useful marker that
you are a long way from anything edible.

## What composts and what does not

For the [composter](../devices/composter.html):

**Rots down:** leaves (both kinds), flowers (both colours), tall grass, crop stalks,
seeds, wheat, potatoes, tomatoes, beans, rice, and **wool**.

**Does not:** raw meat, fish, cooked dishes, cheese, sausages, or anything made of
stone or metal. A farm that could turn its own pork into fertiliser would make the
pig the cheapest crop in the game, and a cooked dish is worth too much to rot.
