| | Recipe | Gives |
|---|---|---|
| **Fence** | 2 planks + 4 sticks | **3 fences** |
| **Fence gate** | 2 planks + 4 sticks | 1 gate |

Both at a [crafting table](crafting-table.html). Both break with an axe, quickly, and both
come apart again on a [disassembly bench](disassembly-bench.html).

## Why they are worth more than the wood in them

Six blocks of material for three fence panels looks expensive for what is, structurally,
a stick. What you are buying is **an animal that stays where you put it**.

[Animals do not respawn](../animals/index.html#animals-are-generated-with-the-land) in
SynthMiner. A herd is placed when the land is generated and nothing ever appears later. So
the two animals you have penned in are, permanently, your entire supply of pork or milk or
wool — and the ones wandering loose are the ones you will eventually lose track of.

**Build a pen on your first day in a place you mean to stay.** Two of each animal in it is
a farm.

## The gate

A gate is a fence you can walk through. Press <kbd>E</kbd> to open it and again to close
it; it opens for you and stays shut for livestock.

A pen wants exactly one gate. More than one is more ways for the herd to be somewhere
else when you get back.

## Building a pen

- Fences are **taller than they look** — you cannot jump one, and neither can a cow.
- Corners matter: a fence line with a gap in it is not a fence.
- Animals **shove** each other and you. A herd crowding a gateway will push you out of it,
  so open the gate, step in, and close it behind you.
- Put the pen next to your [wheat field](../plants/wheat.html) if you keep cows or sheep,
  and next to your potatoes or beans if you keep pigs. You will be feeding them to breed
  them.

## Getting a herd in

The easy way: **hold their food.** An animal follows whoever is carrying what it eats,
from up to 7 blocks away — so walk to the pen with a potato in hand and the pigs come with
you.

A fed animal stops following, because it has gone off looking for a partner instead. So
lead them in *first*, then feed them.
