| **Recipe** | **9 iron ingots**, at a [crafting table](crafting-table.html) |
| **Slots** | **two** inputs · sausage out · **bone** out |
| **Speed** | **1 minute of playing** per sausage |
| **Breaks with** | A pickaxe — required |

The most expensive machine in the game, and the only one that is worth nine ingots.

## The two input slots

This is the question everybody asks. **The second slot is for the flower.**

| Recipe | Slot A | Slot B | Out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sausage** | 1 pork | 1 **red or yellow flower** | 1 sausage |
| **Vegetarian sausage** | 2 beans | — | 1 fake sausage |

A sausage is *meat and a flower*. Put pork in with nothing in the second slot and the
machine will sit there doing nothing — so it tells you, in as many words, that it needs a
red or a yellow flower as well.

Either colour works. They make the same sausage.

<p class="note"><b>Yellow flowers can be grown.</b> Throw <a
href="composter.html">compost</a> on a grass block and a 3×3 patch of yellow flowers
comes up. That is what stops a sausage farm being limited by how much grassland you have
walked across. Red flowers cannot be grown — but since either colour works, it does not
matter.</p>

## The vegetarian one

**Two beans, no flower.** It is worth exactly what the pork sausage is worth — 2 hunger,
2 saturation — and it **counts as a pizza's sausage**, so you can make the best food in
the game without killing anything.

Put beans in while flowers are sitting in the other slot and **the flowers are handed
straight back to you**, because a bean sausage has no use for them. If your pack is full
they stay in the machine, where they are doing no harm.

## The bone

The second *output* slot is for bones.

> Roughly **one pork sausage in six** leaves a **bone** behind.

A bone is the only thing that [tames a dog](../animals/dog.html), and there is no other
source of one anywhere in the game. A **bean** sausage never leaves one — the bone comes
off the meat.

So: **dog = pig + flower + patience.** Keep the machine fed and check the bone slot.

A bone that has nowhere to go is lost rather than held — the machine is not a chest and
will not stop working over one — so empty the bone slot now and then.

## Taking things out

Every slot, input and output, has a **"take it out"** row at the top of its list. Pick
that and the contents come back to you. Breaking the machine also drops everything
inside it.

## It never runs while you are away

Like every machine here, the sausage maker works out what should have happened the moment
you open it — so a machine left loaded is simply done when you come back, and one in a
chunk you have not visited costs nothing.

A minute a sausage is a minute of real playing time while you are awake. **A night's sleep
finishes the whole batch**, because sleeping winds the world's clock forward by up to a
day. Load it up before bed. See [Time, growth and sleeping](../mechanics/time.html).
