---
title: Disassembly bench
summary: Takes crafted things apart and gives the materials back. Not in Minecraft.
---

| | |
|---|---|
| **Recipe** | **4 planks + 1 coal**, at a [crafting table](crafting-table.html) |
| **Breaks with** | An axe |

Point at it and press <kbd>E</kbd>. It shows you what in your inventory can be taken
apart, and does it.

## What it undoes

Most things. A crafted item is **reversible** unless taking it apart would create
something out of nothing, and the recipe list marks which is which.

**Reversible** — you get the materials back:

- every tool: pickaxes, axes, shovels, hoes, shears, the fishing rod
- the bucket
- the crafting table, chest, trash chest, this bench
- the furnace, composter, cheese maker, sausage maker, kitchen stove
- fences, gates, the bed

**Not reversible:**

| | Because |
|---|---|
| Planks and sticks | They are basic materials; there is nothing underneath them |
| Torches | A torch coming back as coal and a stick would be a way of un-burning it |
| Iron ingots | An ingot does not go back to ore |
| Glass, stone, coal from the furnace | Same reason — smelting is one-way |
| Tomato seeds | Two seeds must not become a whole tomato |
| String | Three string must not become a fleece, or wool and string would be the same thing at different rates |
| Cheese, sausages, cooked dishes | Food is not unmade |

## Why you want one

**Iron.** A stone pickaxe you have finished with is 3 cobblestone, which is nothing —
but an iron pickaxe is 3 ingots, and those are three ingots you could spend on a bucket
instead. The bench means iron spent on the wrong thing is not iron lost.

It also makes experimenting cheap. Built the sausage maker in the wrong place? Break it,
and it comes back as a sausage maker item you can put down elsewhere — and if you have
gone off the whole idea, the bench turns it back into **nine iron ingots**.

<p class="note"><b>Breaking a machine already gives you the machine back.</b> The bench
is for when you want the <em>materials</em> instead — which is almost always about
getting iron out of something.</p>

## The coal

Four planks and **one coal**, the same as the [trash chest](trash-chest.html): the two
machines that take things apart both cost a little fire to build.
