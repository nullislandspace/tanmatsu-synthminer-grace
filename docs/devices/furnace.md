---
title: Furnace
summary: Eight cobblestone. Ore into metal, sand into glass, and logs into coal.
---

| | |
|---|---|
| **Recipe** | **8 cobblestone**, at a [crafting table](crafting-table.html) |
| **Breaks with** | A pickaxe — **required**, or it drops nothing |
| **Slots** | Input, fuel, output |
| **Speed** | **10 seconds** per item |

## What it smelts

| In | Out | Why you want it |
|---|---|---|
| **Iron ore** | 1 iron ingot | The whole reason to go underground |
| **Sand** | 1 glass | The only source of glass in the game |
| **Cobblestone** | 1 stone | Stone is a nicer building block, and the stove wants 6 of it |
| **1 log** | 1 coal | Coal without a mine |

That is the complete list. Note that food is **not** on it — cooking happens on a
[kitchen stove](kitchen-stove.html), not in a furnace.

## Fuel

The fuel slot is separate from the input, so fuel is never an ingredient.

| Fuel | Burns for | Items smelted |
|---|---|---|
| **Coal** | 1600 ticks | **8** |
| A log, planks, or any wooden block | 300 ticks | 1½ |
| A wooden tool (worn out or not) | 200 ticks | 1 |
| A stick | 100 ticks | ½ |

Anything made of wood burns: a crafting table, a chest, a trash chest, a disassembly
bench, a composter, a birch log. Spent wooden tools are fuel too, which is why nothing
is ever really wasted.

<p class="note"><b>Coal is worth more than five logs as fuel.</b> So if you have coal,
burn coal. If you have no coal, smelt a log into one — a log burned gives 300 ticks, a
log <em>smelted</em> gives you a coal worth 1600. Burn your worst wood to convert your
best wood into coal.</p>

## It never runs while you are away

The furnace does not tick. It remembers when you last looked at it and works out what
should have happened when you open it, so a furnace left full of ore and coal is simply
*done* when you come back. Nothing is lost by walking off.

It does not bank time it could not use, though: a furnace with fuel and nothing to smelt
does not save the fuel up. Put the ore in first.

## Getting things out

Open it and take from the output slot. Breaking the furnace also drops everything inside
it — including whatever was mid-smelt.

There is **no "take it out" row** on a furnace's slots yet, so if you put the wrong thing
in the input, breaking and replacing the furnace is the way to get it back. It costs you
nothing; the furnace drops.
