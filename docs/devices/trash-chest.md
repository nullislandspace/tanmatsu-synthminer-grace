---
title: Trash chest
summary: A chest that destroys what you put in it. Ten minutes later, which is the merciful part.
---

| | |
|---|---|
| **Recipe** | **8 planks + 1 coal**, at a [crafting table](crafting-table.html) |
| **Holds** | 27 stacks, temporarily |
| **Empties** | **10 minutes of playing** after you last put something in |
| **Breaks with** | An axe |

## What it is for

The game gives you a lot of things you do not want. Dirt from a foundation, gravel,
cobblestone you have no use for, sixty sardines. A trash chest is where those go.

It works like a [chest](chest.html) — open it, put things in — except that ten minutes
later everything in it is gone for good.

<p class="note"><b>Ten minutes of <em>playing</em>, not ten minutes of world time.</b>
Those are different clocks: an in-game day is only twenty minutes of playing, so ten
minutes of world time would be about three seconds. The bin gives you ten real
minutes.</p>

## The ten minutes are the point

One clock for the whole bin, and it **restarts every time you put something in**. So the
rule is simply: *it empties ten minutes after you last threw something away.*

Which means you can change your mind. Threw out the wrong stack? Open it and take it
back. That is the whole reason it is not instant.

<p class="note"><b>Sleeping empties it.</b> A night in a <a href="bed.html">bed</a> winds
the world's clock forward by up to a whole day, and the bin's ten minutes go with it. So
anything sitting in a trash chest when you go to bed is gone in the morning, whether its
ten minutes were up or not.</p>

## The coal

Eight planks and **one coal** — the same "plus a little fire" that the
[disassembly bench](disassembly-bench.html) costs. Both of them destroy things, and the
design is that the destructive machines cost a little fire to build.

## It is not a furnace

A trash chest does not give you anything for what you put in. If what you want is to
turn surplus into something useful:

- **plant matter** goes in a [composter](composter.html) and comes out as compost and
  worms;
- **crafted things** go in a [disassembly bench](disassembly-bench.html) and come out as
  their materials;
- **wooden things** go in a [furnace](furnace.html) and burn as fuel.

The trash chest is for what is left after all three.
