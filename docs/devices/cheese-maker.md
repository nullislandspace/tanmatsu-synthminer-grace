---
title: Cheese maker
summary: A barrel. Milk in, cheese out, a day later — and the bucket comes straight back.
---

| | |
|---|---|
| **Recipe** | **7 planks**, at a [crafting table](crafting-table.html) |
| **Takes** | 1 **bucket of milk** |
| **Gives** | 1 **cheese** |
| **Speed** | **1 in-game day** — 20 minutes of playing, or one night's sleep |
| **Breaks with** | An axe |

## Using it

1. Milk a [cow](../animals/cow.html) with a [bucket](../tools/bucket.html).
2. Put the bucket of milk in the barrel.
3. **You get the empty pail back immediately** — not in a day's time.
4. Come back tomorrow, or sleep, and take the cheese out.

The bucket coming straight back matters. The barrel keeps the milk *bucket* as its token
of what is standing in it, but the pail itself is yours again at once — so one bucket can
keep several barrels going, instead of being locked up for a whole day.

## You can see what it holds

The barrel is drawn differently depending on what is in it: empty, full of milk, or
holding finished cheese. So you can tell from across the room which ones need emptying,
without opening any of them.

## What cheese is worth

| | |
|---|---|
| **Eaten** | 2 hunger, 2 saturation — no cooking needed |
| **Pizza** | 1 cheese is one of the four things a [pizza](../food.html) wants |

The pizza is the best food in the game — 10 hunger and 8 saturation — and cheese is the
part of it that needs a cow.

Cheese does not compost, and the [disassembly bench](disassembly-bench.html) will not
turn it back into milk.

## Build several

One barrel is one cheese a day. If you are aiming at pizzas you will want a few of them,
and at seven planks each that is under two logs apiece.

The fastest way to run them is to fill every barrel and then go to
[bed](bed.html) — a night's sleep is a whole in-game day, so they are all done in the
morning.

## Taking something out

Every slot has a **"take it out"** row in its list, so a bucket of milk you put in by
mistake can come back. Breaking the barrel also drops everything inside it.
