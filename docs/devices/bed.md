| | |
|---|---|
| **Recipe** | **3 wool + 3 planks**, at a [crafting table](crafting-table.html) |
| **Takes up** | **Two blocks** — put it down and it lies out in the direction you are facing |
| **Breaks with** | Anything; an axe is quicker. Either half takes the whole bed |

The most useful thing in the game for what it costs, and the reason to keep
[sheep](../animals/sheep.html).

## The two things it does

### 1. It sets where you respawn

Press <kbd>E</kbd> on it — **day or night** — and that bed becomes home. When you die you
wake up there instead of wherever the world first put you, and the **HOME mark on the
compass** moves to it.

This works in broad daylight. If it is not night the bed says so, but the spawn point has
already moved.

### 2. At night, it sleeps through to morning

Press <kbd>E</kbd> on it after dark and the clock winds forward to the next morning. Not a
fixed amount — *to morning*, so going to bed at four in the morning does not put the sun
back where it was.

## Sleeping is the fast-forward button

This is worth understanding properly, because it is the most useful trick in the game.

Sleeping moves the **world's clock** forward by up to a whole day, and nearly everything
that takes time is measured against that clock. So one night's sleep:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Grows your crops** a full day — wheat and tomatoes go seed to ripe overnight |
| **Grows your saplings** into trees |
| **Finishes every composter** — a day is exactly one unit |
| **Finishes every cheese** |
| **Finishes the sausage maker and the stove**, for as long as their fuel and ingredients last |
| **Finishes whatever is in the furnace**, for as long as its fuel lasts |
| **Empties the trash chest** |

What it does **not** touch is anything to do with **animals or your own body**. Those run on
a count of ticks you actually played, not on the world's clock, so a night's sleep does
**not**:

- make you hungry — sleeping is strictly free;
- regrow a shorn sheep's fleece;
- grow a calf up, or wear off a fed animal's mood.

<p class="note"><b>So the rhythm of a farm is: load everything, then go to bed.</b> Fill
the composters, fill the barrels, put coal and ingredients in the stove, and sleep. It is
all waiting for you in the morning.</p>

## Notes

- The **foot** of the bed is where you wake up, whichever end you used.
- Breaking either half takes the whole bed down and drops one bed.
- The bed is **not** fuel — wool does not burn — but it is reversible on a
  [disassembly bench](disassembly-bench.html), which gives the 3 wool and 3 planks back.
- There is no monster in this version of the game, so sleeping through the night is a
  convenience rather than a safety measure. It is still the single best thing you can do
  with the dark.
