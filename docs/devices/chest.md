title: Chest
summary: Eight planks, twenty-seven stacks.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Recipe** | **8 planks**, at a [crafting table](crafting-table.html) |
| **Holds** | **27 stacks** |
| **Breaks with** | An axe (2.5 s by hand, 0.6 s with a stone axe) |

Point at it and press <kbd>E</kbd> to open it. Move things between it and your
inventory.

**Breaking a chest drops everything in it.** You cannot lose a chestful of iron to a
misplaced swing — the contents come out on the ground first, and then the chest does.

## Worth knowing

- Chests are cheap. Two logs is a chest. Build more of them than you think you need and
  sort by kind; the inventory is small and the game gives you a great many different
  things.
- A chest is **fuel**. It burns in a furnace for 300 ticks, like the planks it is made
  of. So is a crafting table, a composter and a trash chest.
- The [disassembly bench](disassembly-bench.html) turns a chest back into 8 planks.

## The stove's chest

A [kitchen stove](kitchen-stove.html) comes with a chest of its own — the stove's recipe
includes the eight planks for it, and putting the stove down puts **both** blocks in
place, chest to the left as you face it.

That chest is an ordinary chest in every way except one: **the stove cooks out of it.**
Tip your farm's produce in and the stove takes what it needs. You can also just use it
as a chest.

Breaking either half of a stove-and-chest pair takes both down, and gives you back
everything in the chest plus the stove item.
