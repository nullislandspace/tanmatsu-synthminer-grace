title: Torches
summary: One coal and one stick makes four. The cheapest important thing in the game.
---

| | |
|---|---|
| **Recipe** | **1 coal + 1 stick → 4 torches** — with **bare hands**, no table needed |
| **Light** | 14 of a possible 15 — as bright as it gets |
| **Breaks** | Instantly, and it comes back |

## Where they go

Point at the top of a block and press <kbd>E</kbd> to stand one up, or at the **side** of
a block to hang one on the wall. Wall torches are what you want in a tunnel — a torch on
the floor is a torch you kick.

Breaking a torch gives it back, so there is no cost to moving one.

## Use a lot of them

Four torches for one coal and one stick is nothing, and one coal also buys you eight
smelts in a furnace — but coal is not the constraint here. Light is the difference between
a cave you can work in and a hole you fall down, and a fall of more than three blocks
hurts.

Place them as you dig, on the same wall every time. You are laying a trail as much as
lighting the tunnel: follow the torches and you get out.

## They cannot be un-burnt

A torch is one of the few crafted things the
[disassembly bench](disassembly-bench.html) will **not** take apart, because getting the
coal back out of a torch would be a way of un-burning it. The stick is gone too.

At four to a coal, nobody has ever minded.
