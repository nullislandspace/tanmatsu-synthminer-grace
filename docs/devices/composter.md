| | |

| **Recipe** | **7 planks**, at a [crafting table](crafting-table.html) |
| **Slots** | Input · **compost** out · **worms** out |
| **Speed** | **1 in-game day** per unit — 20 minutes of playing, or one night's sleep |
| **Fuel** | None. Rotting needs no fire |
| **Breaks with** | An axe |

The composter is the quiet centre of the whole farm, and it does two completely
different jobs out of one box.

## What goes in

Plant matter, and food you would rather not eat.

**Rots down:** leaves (both kinds), red and yellow flowers, tall grass, crop stalks,
wheat, wheat seeds, tomatoes, tomato seeds, potatoes, beans, rice, and **wool**.

**Does not:** raw meat, fish, cooked dishes, cheese, sausages, or anything made of stone
or metal.

That list is mostly **what you clear anyway**. Felling a tree gives you a canopy of
leaves; tilling a field means pulling up grass and flowers. The composter is a use for
the by-product rather than another thing to farm.

## What comes out

Each unit gives:

- **1 compost**, and
- **0, 1 or 2 worms** — rolled from the world itself, so the same box at the same point
  in its life always gives the same number.

Zero is a real answer and has to be: a box that always paid out would make worms as free
as scraps.

### Compost

Compost is fertiliser, and it does two things:

| Use it on | And |
|---|---|
| **A growing crop** | It jumps **one whole stage** — so three lots of compost take a plant from seed to ripe on the spot |
| **The top of a grass block** | A **3×3 patch of yellow flowers** grows around it |

The flowers matter because a [sausage](sausage-maker.html) needs one, and this is the
only way to *make* flowers rather than find them. See
[Flowers](../plants/flowers.html).

### Worms

Worms are **fishing bait**, and the only bait there is. One worm per cast — see
[Fishing](../mechanics/fishing.html).

This is the connection worth noticing: **the farm feeds the fishing.** The leaves you
clear become the worms that catch the salmon that goes in the sashimi. Nothing else in
the game links the two.

## It never runs while you are away

Like every machine here, the composter does not tick. It works out what should have
happened when you open it — so a box left for a week is simply full, and a box in a chunk
you have not visited costs nothing.

An in-game day is 20 minutes of playing, **or one night's sleep**. Sleeping is by far the
fastest way to run a composter: fill it, go to bed, and it is done in the morning.

## Build several

One composter is one unit a day, which is not much. Compost and worms are both things you
want a steady supply of, and seven planks is under two logs. Build four.
