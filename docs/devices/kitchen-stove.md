| | |
|---|---|
| **Recipe** | **3 iron ingots + 6 stone + 8 planks**, at a [crafting table](crafting-table.html) |
| **Puts down** | **Two blocks** — the stove, and a chest beside it |
| **Slots** | Dish · fuel · output |
| **Speed** | **1 minute of playing** per dish |
| **Breaks with** | Anything. A pickaxe is quicker |

In Minecraft, food is prepared on a crafting table or in a furnace. That makes no sense,
so SynthMiner has a stove.

<p class="note"><b>Six <em>stone</em>, not cobblestone.</b> You have to smelt the
cobblestone first — 6 cobble through a <a href="furnace.html">furnace</a> gives you the
6 stone. It catches everybody once.</p>

## It cooks out of the chest

This is the whole idea, and it is what makes the stove different from every other machine
in the game: **the stove has no input slots.** What goes into a dish is whatever is in
the chest next to it.

A pizza wants four different things and a kebab wants four. Filling four input slots by
hand, per dish, is a chore. Tipping your farm's whole produce into one chest and telling
the stove "cook this" is not.

So the working pattern is: **harvest everything into the stove's chest, then pick a dish.**

## The chest comes with it

The stove's recipe includes the eight planks for the chest, and **putting the stove down
puts both blocks in place** — chest on the left as you face it. There is no second thing
to carry and no "which chest?" to get wrong.

It is all or nothing: if there is no room for both blocks, neither goes down.

**Breaking either half takes both down**, and gives you back everything in the chest plus
the stove item. The chest half does not drop a separate chest.

## Using it

Point at the stove and press <kbd>E</kbd>. Three rows:

| Row | |
|---|---|
| **Dish** | Pick from the list of thirteen. Each shows what it needs and what it is worth |
| **Fuel** | Coal, or anything wooden — the same fuels a [furnace](furnace.html) burns |
| **Output** | Where the finished dishes stack up |

Then it cooks, one dish a minute, for as long as there is fuel and there are ingredients.

**Ingredients are taken when a dish finishes, not when it starts** — so a stove you
interrupt has not eaten anything.

## When it will not start

It tells you which of these it is, which saves a lot of guessing:

| It says | Fix |
|---|---|
| No dish chosen | Pick one |
| No chest | Put the pair down properly, or replace a broken chest half |
| **Missing an ingredient** — and it names it | Put some in the chest |
| No fuel | Coal, logs, planks, sticks, spent wooden tools |
| Output full | Empty the output slot |

## The thirteen dishes

Cheapest first, which is roughly the order they appear in.

| Dish | Needs | Hunger | Saturation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Baked potato | 1 potato | 1 | 1 |
| Grilled tomatoes | 1 tomato | 2 | — |
| Baked beans | 1 bean | 2 | — |
| Bread | 3 wheat | 2 | 1 |
| Rice patty | 2 rice | 3 | 1 |
| **Smoked salmon** | 1 salmon | 2 | **4** |
| Grilled shrimp | 3 shrimp | 2 | 2 |
| Sashimi | 1 salmon + 1 rice | 4 | — |
| Pork and beans | 1 pork + 1 bean | 5 | 2 |
| Steak and potatoes | 1 beef + 1 potato | 5 | 2 |
| Mutton mash | 1 mutton + 2 potatoes | 5 | 2 |
| **Kebab** | 1 mutton + 1 bean + 2 wheat + 1 tomato | **8** | **7** |
| **Pizza** | 2 wheat + 1 sausage + 1 cheese + 2 shrimp | **10** | **8** |

Read the table down and it is a ladder: one crop cooked alone is worth 1 or 2, two things
together are worth 4 or 5, and the two dishes that want four different ingredients are
worth 8 and 10.

The pizza appears **twice** in the picker — once for a real sausage and once for the
vegetarian one. Either row cooks the same pizza, and if both kinds are in the chest it
uses whichever it finds first.

**Smoked salmon is the odd one and is meant to be:** only 2 hunger but **4 saturation**,
which is more reserve per drumstick than anything else in the game. It is what you carry
when travelling. The pizza is what you eat when you are starving.

See [Every food](../food.html) and [Hunger and saturation](../mechanics/hunger.html).

## Fuel

One coal burns for 1600 ticks and a dish takes 1200, so **one coal is one dish and a
bit**. A long cooking session wants a handful of coal in the fuel slot, or a stack of
planks.

A minute a dish is a minute of real playing time while you are awake — but the stove runs
on the same clock as everything else, so **a night's sleep finishes everything the stove
has fuel and ingredients for.** Load it up before bed. See
[Time, growth and sleeping](../mechanics/time.html).

<p class="note"><b>The fuel slot has no "take it out" row.</b> If you put the wrong thing
in it, break the stove and put it down again — you lose nothing, both blocks come back and
so does everything in the chest.</p>
