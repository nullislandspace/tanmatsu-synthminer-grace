---
title: The five biomes
summary: Where each one is, what grows there, and what lives in it.
---

Which biome you are standing in is decided by two slow noise fields —
temperature and humidity — and not by the height of the ground, so the same
biome turns up on hilltops and in valleys. One is a few minutes across on foot:
far enough to feel like somewhere, near enough that you can find another.

The **ground** blends smoothly between them, so there are no cliffs at a border.
The **surface block** changes at a line, the way grass meets sand in every game
that has both.

| Biome | Share of the world | Ground | Trees | Wild crop |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Plains** | about four fifths | Grass over 3–5 of dirt, gently rolling | The odd one | **Potato**, very rare |
| **Forest** | uncommon | Grass, a little deeper | Thick — you walk between them in shade | **Beans**, rare |
| **Birch wood** | rare | Grass | Thick, and all white trunks | **Tomato**, the commonest of the three |
| **Mountains** | about an eighth | Grass over only 1–3 of dirt; steep | Thin | — |
| **Sand flats** | uncommon | Sand over sand, sandstone below | None | — |

## Plains

Most of the world. Open, rolling grassland with scattered oaks, red and yellow
flowers and tall grass. This is where you will spend your time and where you
should build.

**Lives here:** cows, pigs and sheep — about one chunk in 25 holds a herd of each,
so you meet *something* every eight chunks or so. Also the occasional lost dog,
about one chunk in a hundred.

**Grows here:** the wild **potato** — about one column in seven thousand, so one
or two in a plain you can see across. It is worth stopping for: a potato is both
a crop seed and pig food.

## Forest

Oaks close enough together that the light changes. More undergrowth than flowers.
Good for wood, awkward for building.

**Lives here:** pigs commonly, cows sometimes, and **wild dogs** — a forest is
the best place in the world to look for one.

**Grows here:** wild **beans**. Beans are the only crop with no raw form at all
and they are what a vegetarian sausage is made of.

## Birch wood

Thick white trunks and pale green leaves. Deliberately rare — the point of it is
that you come across one, not that you can always see one.

**Grows here:** wild **tomatoes**, which need a crafting table before they are a
crop at all (one tomato makes two tomato seeds). Tomatoes are also the only thing
in the game you can eat straight off the plant.

**Lives here:** pigs, and dogs more often than the plains.

## Mountains

Cold, high and steep, with thin soil. Above **y = 40** the grass gives up and it
is bare stone; above 41, about a fifth of it is under **snow**.

Mountains are the quickest way to reach coal without digging, because the ground
itself gets up to where the veins are.

**Lives here:** sheep, and nothing else.

## Sand flats

Sand all the way down, with sandstone about five blocks under the surface.
**Flat** — which is most of what makes it read as a desert rather than as pale
grassland. Nothing grows except **cactus**, in columns one to three tall.

No animals at all. Nothing to eat. Sand is worth carrying home though: a furnace
turns it into glass.

## Everywhere: the shore

The waterline is **sand in every biome**, so every coast is a beach — including
the ones in the middle of a mountain range. That is also the only place **rice**
grows wild: in water exactly one block deep, standing on sand, with air above it.
