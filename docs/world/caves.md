---
title: Caves and the deep
summary: Where the tunnels are, how to find one, and how not to die in it.
---

A 3D noise field carves tunnels through the stone as the world is generated. They
are **long worms rather than round bubbles**, and they get **wider the deeper they
go** — the shallow world is cramped and the deep world is open.

## Finding one

Most caves never touch the surface. A separate field decides where one is allowed
to, and it opens the mouth up wider than the tunnel behind it, so an entrance is
something you walk into rather than something you fall down.

About **1 in 90 columns** of the world has a cave open at the surface. In practice
that means: if you are looking for one, walk a ridge line and look down slopes.
Mountains are the best odds, because there is more vertical rock face.

The alternative is to make your own. A staircase down is slower but it cannot be
lost, and it is the only way to be sure you get below **y = 28**, which is where
iron starts.

## Going down

Take, in rough order of how much you will regret not having it:

1. **Torches.** One coal and one stick makes four. Place them as you go — you are
   putting down a trail as much as a light.
2. **A stone pickaxe at least.** Iron ore will not drop for a wooden one.
3. **Food.** Mining costs hunger, and so does climbing back out.
4. **A spare pickaxe**, or the materials for one. 130 blocks goes faster than you
   expect.
5. **A bucket of water.** It is the emergency exit from a drop you did not mean to
   make, and the way down a shaft you do not want to dig stairs into.

## What is down there

| | |
|---|---|
| **Coal ore** | Anywhere up to y = 40. Veins of a handful. |
| **Iron ore** | Only below y = 28, and needs a stone pickaxe. Smaller, rarer veins. |
| **Gravel** | Falls when you dig under it. So does sand. |
| **Water** | Anything hollow below y = 24 that the sea can reach is flooded. |
| **Bedrock** | y = 0, unbreakable. The bottom. |

Nothing is hunting you. This version of the game has no monsters — the danger in
a cave is the drop, the dark, and running out of food a long way from home.

## Falling

Three blocks are free. Every block after that is half a heart, and a fall of
twenty-three is fatal from full health. The fall is measured **from the highest
point your feet have been**, so a ledge half way down really does break it into
two shorter falls.

And water at the bottom cancels the whole thing, however far you came.
