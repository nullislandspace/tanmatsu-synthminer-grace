---
title: Fishing
summary: A rod that never wears out, worms that do, and a bite you have to strike.
---

Fishing is the only source of the three fish, and two of them go in the best dishes in the
game. It is also the one activity you cannot start on your first day.

## What you need

| | |
|---|---|
| A [**fishing rod**](../tools/fishing-rod.html) | 3 sticks + 2 string. Never wears out |
| **Worms** | Out of a [composter](../devices/composter.html) — 0 to 2 per unit |
| **Water** | Any water in reach. A lake, the sea, a pond you dug |

The rod is nearly free. **The cost of fishing is worms**, and worms come out of the same
box that makes your compost — which is the link between the farm and the water, and the
reason a composter is worth building even if you never fertilise anything.

Getting there takes a while: string comes from wool, wool wants
[shears](../tools/shears.html), shears want iron. That is deliberate. Fishing is a
mid-game activity, like the composter that feeds it.

## Casting

**Two taps, not one.**

1. Point at water within reach and press <kbd>E</kbd>. The float goes out and **one worm is
   spent**.
2. **Wait** — somewhere between **4 and 14 seconds**. When something bites, the float dips
   and there is a splash.
3. Press <kbd>E</kbd> again **within about 1.5 seconds** of the bite. That is the strike,
   and it lands the fish.

A cast with no second press would be a button that dispenses food. The waiting is the whole
of what fishing has ever been.

## Missing, and reeling in

| What you did | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Struck on the bite** | You get the fish. The line comes in |
| **Missed the window** | **Nothing is lost.** The line stays out and another fish comes along |
| **Pressed <kbd>E</kbd> early**, before any bite has come | "Too soon" — the line stays out |
| **Pressed <kbd>E</kbd> again after waiting a while** with nothing biting | You reel in, and **the worm is forfeit** |

The worm buys the **cast**, not the fish — so a mistimed strike costs you a moment and
nothing else. One cast can land several fish in a row if you keep hitting the window.

If you are not sure whether something is biting: **wait.** Waiting is free, reeling in is
not.

Each bite is its own independent roll, so a line that has been out a while is no more likely
to produce than a fresh one. It is a wait, not a progress bar.

## What comes up

| Fish | Chance |
|---|---|
| **Sardine** | 50% |
| **Shrimp** | 30% |
| **Salmon** | 20% |

**None of them is food raw.** A [kitchen stove](../devices/kitchen-stove.html) is what makes
them worth catching:

| Dish | Needs | Hunger | Saturation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Smoked salmon** | 1 salmon | 2 | **4** |
| **Sashimi** | 1 salmon + 1 rice | 4 | — |
| **Grilled shrimp** | 3 shrimp | 2 | 2 |
| **Pizza** | **2 shrimp** + 2 wheat + sausage + cheese | **10** | **8** |

**Sardines have no use yet.** They are half of everything you catch and there is nothing to
do with them — compost will not take a fish, and no dish wants one. Put them in a
[trash chest](../devices/trash-chest.html).

## Why bother

Two reasons, and they are good ones.

**Smoked salmon** carries 4 points of saturation for 2 of hunger — more reserve per drumstick
than anything else in the game, including the pizza. It is the thing to fill your pack with
before a long walk. See [Hunger and saturation](hunger.html).

**The pizza needs two shrimp**, and the pizza is the best food there is. There is no way to
make one without fishing.
