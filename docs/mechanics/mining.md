---
title: Mining speed and tools
summary: What every block costs to break, with what, and which ones give you nothing without the right tool.
---

Hold <kbd>Q</kbd> on a block until it breaks. How long that takes depends on the block and
on what is in your hand.

## The multipliers

A tool only helps on **its own class** of block. A pickaxe does nothing for dirt.

| In hand | Speed |
|---|---|
| Bare hands, or the wrong tool | **1×** |
| **Wooden** tool of the right class | **2×** |
| **Stone** | **4×** |
| **Iron** | **6×** |

So an iron pickaxe is six times faster on stone than your fist, and exactly as fast as your
fist on a log.

## Blocks that need a tool

These give you **nothing at all** when broken by hand — the block disappears and you get no
drop. The game tells you what you need before you start.

| Block | Needs |
|---|---|
| Stone, cobblestone, sandstone | **Any pickaxe** |
| Coal ore | **Any pickaxe** |
| Furnace | **Any pickaxe** |
| Sausage maker | **Any pickaxe** |
| **Iron ore** | **Stone pickaxe or better** |
| **Leaves** | **Shears** — anything else destroys them |

Everything else in the game drops for bare hands. A tool just makes it faster.

## Every block, and how long it takes

Seconds. A dash means the tool does not help.

| Block | Hand | Wood | Stone | Iron | Tool |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Flowers, tall grass, crops, saplings, torches | instant | — | — | — | — |
| Snow | 0.5 | 0.25 | 0.13 | 0.08 | Shovel |
| Leaves | 0.4 | 0.2 | — | — | **Shears** *(the only thing they drop for)* |
| Sand | 0.75 | 0.4 | 0.2 | 0.1 | Shovel |
| Gravel | 0.9 | 0.45 | 0.2 | 0.15 | Shovel |
| Dirt, grass, farmland | 1.0 | 0.5 | 0.25 | 0.17 | Shovel |
| Bed | 1.0 | 0.5 | 0.25 | 0.17 | Axe |
| Cactus | 0.6 | — | — | — | — |
| Glass | 0.6 | — | — | — | — |
| Log, planks, birch log | 2.0 | 1.0 | 0.5 | 0.33 | Axe |
| Fence, gate, sign | 2.0 | 1.0 | 0.5 | 0.33 | Axe |
| Crafting table, chest, trash chest, bench, composter, cheese maker, stove's chest | 2.5 | 1.25 | 0.6 | 0.4 | Axe |
| Sausage maker | 4.5 | 2.25 | 1.1 | 0.75 | **Pickaxe** |
| Sandstone | 6.0 | 3.0 | 1.5 | 1.0 | **Pickaxe** |
| Stone | 7.5 | 3.75 | **1.9** | 1.25 | **Pickaxe** |
| Cobblestone | 8.0 | 4.0 | 2.0 | 1.3 | **Pickaxe** |
| Kitchen stove | 9.0 | 4.5 | 2.25 | 1.5 | Pickaxe *(not required)* |
| Furnace | 9.0 | 4.5 | 2.25 | 1.5 | **Pickaxe** |
| Coal ore | 10.0 | 5.0 | 2.5 | 1.7 | **Pickaxe** |
| Iron ore | 11.0 | 5.5 | **2.8** | 1.8 | **Pickaxe (stone+)** |
| Bedrock, water | never | | | | |

**Stone at 1.9 seconds with a stone pickaxe** is the number the early game is paced around.
By hand it is seven and a half seconds and gives you nothing.

## Special breaking rules

### A tree comes down whole

Break any part of a **trunk the world grew** and the entire tree goes — trunk, branches and
leaves — and every log drops, plus **one or two saplings**. One swing, fifty-odd blocks.

**Only the tree you hit.** The fell follows your trunk upwards and clears the canopy around
its top. A neighbouring tree whose branches touch yours keeps all of its logs.

Logs **you** placed are not part of a tree and come down one at a time, so a log cabin does
not collapse when you take out a wall.

See [Trees and saplings](../plants/trees.html).

### A cactus comes down whole

Same rule: break any part of the column and the lot comes down.

### Sand and gravel fall

Dig out what is holding a pile of sand or gravel up and the rest follows. Useful; also a
reason not to tunnel directly upward under a beach.

### A two-block thing breaks as one

The [bed](../devices/bed.html), a rice plant and a [stove and its chest](../devices/kitchen-stove.html)
each take up two cells and break as a single object from either end. You always get the whole
thing back.

## Wear

One point of durability per block broken. 60 for a wooden tool, 130 stone, 220 for shears,
250 iron. The bar under the item in the hotbar shows what is left.

Wooden tools burn in a [furnace](../devices/furnace.html) for 200 ticks, worn out or not, so
a spent wooden pickaxe is fuel rather than rubbish.

See [Tools](../tools/index.html).
