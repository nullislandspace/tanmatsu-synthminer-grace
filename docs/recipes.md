Press <kbd>C</kbd> for the recipe book. There is no grid and no shapes — you pick what you
want from a list, and it tells you what it needs. The book only shows recipes whose
ingredients you have **held at least one of**, so it grows as you play.

**↔ means reversible**: a [disassembly bench](devices/disassembly-bench.html) will take it
apart again and give the materials back.

## In a [furnace](devices/furnace.html)

One item in, one out, 10 seconds each. Fuel goes in its own slot and is never an ingredient.

| Make | From |
|---|---|
| **Iron ingot** | 1 iron ore |
| **Glass** | 1 sand |
| **Stone** | 1 cobblestone |
| **Coal** | 1 log |

None of these is reversible — smelting is one-way.

**Fuel:** coal 1600 ticks (8 items) · any wooden block 300 · a wooden tool 200 · a stick 100.

## In a [cheese maker](devices/cheese-maker.html)

One in-game day per cheese.

| Make | From |
|---|---|
| **Cheese** | 1 bucket of milk — *and the empty pail comes straight back* |

## In a [sausage maker](devices/sausage-maker.html)

One minute of playing per sausage. Two input slots.

| Make | From |
|---|---|
| **Sausage** | 1 pork + 1 **red flower** |
| **Sausage** | 1 pork + 1 **yellow flower** |
| **Vegetarian sausage** | 2 beans |

A pork sausage leaves a **bone** in the second output slot about one time in six. A bean
sausage never does.

## On a [kitchen stove](devices/kitchen-stove.html)

One minute of playing per dish. Ingredients come out of **the chest beside the stove**, not
out of input slots.

| Dish | From | Hunger | Sat. |
|---|---|---|---|
| Baked potato | 1 potato | 1 | 1 |
| Grilled tomatoes | 1 tomato | 2 | — |
| Baked beans | 1 bean | 2 | — |
| Bread | 3 wheat | 2 | 1 |
| Rice patty | 2 rice | 3 | 1 |
| Smoked salmon | 1 salmon | 2 | **4** |
| Grilled shrimp | 3 shrimp | 2 | 2 |
| Sashimi | 1 salmon + 1 rice | 4 | — |
| Pork and beans | 1 pork + 1 bean | 5 | 2 |
| Steak and potatoes | 1 beef + 1 potato | 5 | 2 |
| Mutton mash | 1 mutton + 2 potatoes | 5 | 2 |
| **Kebab** | 1 mutton + 1 bean + 2 wheat + 1 tomato | **8** | **7** |
| **Pizza** | 2 wheat + 1 sausage + 1 cheese + 2 shrimp | **10** | **8** |

The pizza appears twice in the picker — once for a real sausage, once for the vegetarian one.
Either makes the same pizza.

See [Every food](food.html) for what those numbers mean.

## Not a recipe: where things come from

Some things are not crafted at all.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Wheat seeds** | Break tall grass |
| **Potatoes, beans, tomatoes, rice** | Wild plants, in their own biomes |
| **Saplings** | Fell a tree — 1 or 2 per tree |
| **Compost, worms** | A [composter](devices/composter.html) |
| **Wool** | [Shears](tools/shears.html) on a sheep |
| **Milk** | A [bucket](tools/bucket.html) on a cow |
| **Pork, beef, mutton** | Killing the animal |
| **Bone** | A pork sausage, 1 in 6 |
| **Sardine, salmon, shrimp** | [Fishing](mechanics/fishing.html) |
| **Yellow flowers** | Compost thrown on grass — a 3×3 patch |
| **Coal, iron ore** | Digging. See [Ores](ores/index.html) |
