**Hunger** and **saturation** are in **points**. Two points is one drumstick, and the bar
holds 20 points — ten drumsticks.

Saturation is an **invisible reserve** that is spent before your drumsticks are, and it is
capped at whatever your hunger currently is. So the **total** column is what a food is
really worth to somebody who is nearly full, and the **hunger** column is what it is worth
to somebody who is nearly empty. See [Hunger and saturation](mechanics/hunger.html).

## What to eat when

**Starving, nearly empty bar** — the **pizza**, or failing that the **kebab**. Ten points of
hunger is half the bar in one bite, and nothing else comes close. This is what they are for.

**Travelling, bar nearly full** — **smoked salmon**. Two points of hunger and four of
reserve is the most walking per item in the game, better even than the pizza's ratio, and one
salmon makes one. Fill your pack with it before a long trip.

**At home, all day** — whatever your farm makes most of. **Pork and beans**, **steak and
potatoes** and **mutton mash** are all 5/2 from two common ingredients and are the
workhorses.

**Day one, no stove** — a **raw tomato**, or **milk** if you have a bucket and have found a
cow. Those are the only two things in the game you can eat without a machine.

## The honest verdicts

**Bread is bad.** Three wheat for 2 hunger and 1 saturation is the worst return in the game.
Grow wheat for the pizza, the kebab, and for feeding cows and sheep — not for bread.

**Baked potato is worse.** One potato for 2 total, when the same potato in *steak and
potatoes* is part of a 7. A potato is also a **seed**, which makes eating one actively
wasteful until you have a field.

**Grilled shrimp is a trap.** Three shrimp for 4 total, when two shrimp in a pizza are part
of an 18. Never grill shrimp if you could be making a pizza.

**Sardines are worthless.** Half of everything you catch, and there is no recipe for one and
no dish that wants one. They do not even compost. Throw them away.

**Cheese and sausages are quietly good.** 4 total, no stove and no fuel needed — just a
machine and a day's wait. A cheese barrel and a sausage maker will feed you without you ever
cooking anything.

## Not food

Raw meat and raw fish are **ingredients**, not food. You cannot eat pork, beef, mutton,
salmon, shrimp or a sardine, and you cannot eat raw wheat, rice, or beans.

The only raw things you can eat are a **tomato** and **milk**.

This is why the [kitchen stove](devices/kitchen-stove.html) is the piece of equipment that
changes the game most: before it, food is what you find, and after it, food is what you make.

## Eating

Point at nothing in particular, hold the food, tap <kbd>E</kbd>.

A full player **cannot** eat — it refuses and says so. And if you are pointing at a chest or
a machine, <kbd>E</kbd> opens that instead; look at the floor.
