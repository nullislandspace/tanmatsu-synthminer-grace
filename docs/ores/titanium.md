## The gate, twice over

Titanium is the only ore in SynthMiner that wants an **iron** pickaxe, and it is
found only in the Deep Mine. So it is behind two doors:

> You need a [mine shaft](../devices/mine-shaft.html) to reach it, and an iron
> pickaxe to move it.

That is deliberate. Iron ore is what makes an iron pickaxe possible, and the Deep
Mine is where iron is **three times richer** than upstairs — so the route to
titanium is: mine iron on the surface, build a shaft, mine iron again down there,
make a pickaxe, and only then start looking at the walls for something silver.

**A stone pickaxe will break titanium ore and drop nothing for it.** Twelve
seconds of hitting a block, and it is gone. This is the one mistake down there
that costs a vein.

## Where to look

Same as every other ore in the cave: the ore sits in the floor and the walls at
the height you are already walking at, and what changes the odds is which
district you are in.

| District | How likely |
|---|---|
| **Lava Sea** | **Most likely** — and the richest iron, too |
| **Cavern** | Occasional |
| **Hills** | Occasional, a little rarer |
| **Low Hall** | Rare |

Same shape as every other vein in the game: **lumpy blobs on a coarse grid**, not
scatter. When you see one block of it there are one or two more beside it.

## Telling it apart

Down there you will see four veins in the same wall, and the one you must not
mistake for another is this one:

| Ore | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **Coal ore** | Stone with black patches |
| **Iron ore** | Stone with **round pale tan** blobs |
| **Diamond ore** | Stone with **round pale blue** crystals |
| **Titanium ore** | Stone with **angular silver** chips, brighter along one edge |

Titanium is the only one of the four drawn as flat-sided shapes rather than
round blobs. That is the tell, and it is worth learning.

## What it is for

**Smelt it, then make tools.** One titanium ore in a furnace gives one
**titanium ingot**, and ingots make the tier‑4 tools:

| Make | From |
|---|---|
| **Titanium pickaxe** | 3 titanium ingots + 2 sticks |
| **Titanium axe** | 3 titanium ingots + 2 sticks |
| **Titanium shovel** | 1 titanium ingot + 2 sticks |

All three are reversible at a [disassembly bench](../devices/disassembly-bench.html)
— the metal comes back, the ore does not.

| Tier | Uses | Digging speed | Damage (axe) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wooden** | 60 | 2× | 3 |
| **Stone** | 130 | 4× | 4 |
| **Iron** | 250 | 6× | 5 |
| **Titanium** | **400** | **8×** | **6** |

There is **no titanium hoe**. The hoe is not a digging tool — it tills — and a
tier for it would buy nothing a farm needs.

<p class="note"><b>Titanium is not a weapon.</b> A titanium axe is the hardest
thing you can swing in this game, at 6 damage, but it is still an axe and not a
sword. Hostile mobs are a matter of light and distance, not of gear — see
<a href="../mobs/index.html">Hostile mobs</a>.</p>

## How it compares to diamond

| | **Diamond** | **Titanium** |
|---|---|---|
| Where | Deep Mine only | Deep Mine only |
| How rare | 0.06% of the rock | **0.05%** |
| Tool | Stone pickaxe or better | **Iron pickaxe or better** |
| Drops | The gem, ready | The ore block, smelt it |
| What it becomes | Nothing yet | **Tools** |

They are rolled independently, so a cave can hold both in the same wall — and
where both land on the same cell, the diamond wins.
