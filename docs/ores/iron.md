| | |
|---|---|
| **Found** | Only below **y = 28** |
| **Needs** | A **stone** pickaxe or better — a wooden one will not do it |
| **Drops** | 1 **iron ore block**; smelt it for an ingot |
| **Vein** | Rarer and tighter than coal: a fifth of the grid cells, under 2 blocks across |
| **Break time** | 11 s by hand *(nothing)*, 2.8 s stone, 1.8 s iron |

## Where to look

**Below 28**, and that is the whole difficulty. Sea level is 24, so iron starts
just about at the depth of the sea floor and goes down from there. You are not
going to stumble on it while landscaping.

Two ways down:

- **A cave**, if you can find one that goes deep enough. Roughly 1 in 90 columns of
  the world has a cave open at the surface, and the tunnels get wider the deeper
  they go.
- **A staircase you dig yourself.** Slower, but it cannot be lost and it gets you
  to a known depth. Press <kbd>Backspace</kbd> to check.

Take torches. Take food. Take a spare pickaxe.

<p class="note"><b>A wooden pickaxe will break iron ore and give you nothing.</b>
The game knows this and tells you what you need before you waste the swings — but
if you are down there with only wood, you are down there for nothing.</p>

## Smelting it

Iron ore is the only ore that does not drop a usable item directly. Put the ore
block in a [furnace](../devices/furnace.html) with any fuel:

**1 iron ore → 1 iron ingot.**

Ingots do not go back into ore — the [disassembly bench](../devices/disassembly-bench.html)
will undo a great many things but not that one.

## What iron is for

This is why the trip is worth it.

| Thing | Cost | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| [**Bucket**](../tools/bucket.html) | 3 ingots | Water for your fields, and **milk** from a cow |
| [**Shears**](../tools/shears.html) | 2 ingots | **Wool** without killing the sheep — which means a bed |
| [**Kitchen stove**](../devices/kitchen-stove.html) | 3 ingots + 6 stone + 8 planks | Cooked food: thirteen dishes |
| [**Sausage maker**](../devices/sausage-maker.html) | **9 ingots** | Sausages, and the bone that tames a dog |
| **Iron pickaxe / axe / shovel** | 3 ingots + 2 sticks (1 for a shovel) | 250 uses and the fastest digging in the game |
| **Iron hoe** | 2 ingots + 2 sticks | A hoe you stop having to re-make |

The tools are the least of it. The bucket, the shears and the stove are what turn
a mine into a farm.

<p class="note"><b>Budget about 20 ingots</b> for a complete setup: bucket (3),
shears (2), stove (3), sausage maker (9), and an iron pickaxe (3). The sausage maker
is the expensive one and it can wait — nothing else is blocked behind it.</p>
