| | Wooden | Stone | Iron |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Recipe** | **1 plank** + 2 sticks | 1 cobblestone + 2 sticks | 1 iron ingot + 2 sticks |
| **Uses** | 60 | 130 | 250 |
| **Speed** | 2× | 4× | 6× |

Made at a [crafting table](../devices/crafting-table.html). It is the cheapest tool
in the game — one block of material — because a shovel is three quarters handle.

## What it speeds up

Soft ground. Nothing requires a shovel; all of these drop for bare hands too.

| Block | Hand | Wooden | Stone | Iron |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sand | 0.75 s | 0.4 s | 0.2 s | 0.1 s |
| Gravel | 0.9 s | 0.45 s | 0.2 s | 0.15 s |
| Dirt, grass, farmland | 1 s | 0.5 s | **0.25 s** | 0.17 s |
| Snow | 0.5 s | 0.25 s | 0.13 s | 0.08 s |

## Why bother

Because of how much of it there is. Digging a foundation, a staircase down, a moat
round a field or a trench for rice is hundreds of blocks of dirt and sand, and a
stone shovel does it four times as fast as your hands.

For one plank and two sticks, a wooden shovel on your first afternoon is the best
value in the game. Make a stone one as soon as you have cobblestone spare.

<p class="note"><b>Sand and gravel fall.</b> Dig one out from under a pile and the
rest comes down. Useful for clearing a beach, less useful directly above your
head.</p>

## As a weapon

1 + its tier, same as a pickaxe: 2, 3, 4. Use the axe.
