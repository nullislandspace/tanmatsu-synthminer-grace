## Nothing else hurts anything

This is the rule that changed the most when weapons arrived:

**Only a weapon can damage a creature.** A fist, an axe, a pickaxe, a shovel, a hoe,
a shears — all of them do **zero**. You can swing at a cow with a titanium axe and the
swing will not land; the HUD says **"A tool will not hurt it"** and the sound says the
same thing.

That moves meat behind a forge, and a forge behind
[12 diamonds](../ores/diamond.html). It also takes the axe out of being a
dual-purpose item and sends it back to being what it is: the thing that fells
trees. See [Animals](../animals/index.html) and [Hostile mobs](../mobs/index.html).

<p class="note"><b>It is the swing that is gated, not the key.</b> <kbd>Q</kbd> still
swings at anything. What changed is that most things in your hand can no longer land a
blow, so the swing is now a decision about what you are holding rather than a reflex.</p>

## What it takes

| Target | Health | Iron sword (7) | Titanium sword (11) |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Pig](../animals/pig.html), [cow](../animals/cow.html) | 10 | 2 swings | **1** |
| [Sheep](../animals/sheep.html), [dog](../animals/dog.html) | 8 | 2 swings | **1** |
| [Skeleton](../mobs/skeleton.html) | 16 | 3 swings | **2** |
| [Zombie](../mobs/zombie.html) | 20 | 3 swings | **2** |

Against the two things in the [Deep Mine](../world/deep-mine.html) that are trying to
kill you, the sword is not an improvement on a pickaxe — it is the difference between a
fight and a retreat. A zombie hits for **10** (five hearts) a swing, unarmoured, and
kills you in two. Three swings of an iron sword ends it; two of a titanium one does.

**A swing that lands costs the sword one use.** A swing at nothing costs nothing. So an
iron sword is good for about eighty zombies, and a titanium one for about two hundred.

## It digs nothing

A sword has no tool class and no tier. It speeds up no block and it will not harvest
anything that requires a tool — iron ore, titanium ore, stone. Hold a pickaxe to mine,
and switch to the sword when something is coming at you.

Because mining wears a sword exactly like it wears a pickaxe, using one to break stone
is the expensive way to lose a weapon.

## Which one

**Iron first.** Two ingots and a day, and 7 damage already kills everything on the
surface in one or two swings. An iron sword is not an endgame item; it is the point at
which farming animals stops being a chore.

**Titanium is the Deep Mine sword.** 11 damage, 400 swings, and two in-game days in the
forge — a day and a half of sleeping through it. It is worth making before you start
claiming ground underground rather than after something has killed you once.

Armour is the other half of this: see [Armour](armour.html).
