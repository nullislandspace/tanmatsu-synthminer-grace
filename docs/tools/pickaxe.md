## What it is required for

A pickaxe is the only tool in the game that **gates** anything. These blocks give you
nothing at all when broken by hand, however long you hit them:

| Block | Needs |
|---|---|
| Stone, cobblestone, sandstone | **Any** pickaxe |
| Coal ore | **Any** pickaxe |
| Furnace, sausage maker | **Any** pickaxe |
| **Iron ore** | **Stone pickaxe or better** |
| **Titanium ore** | **Iron pickaxe or better** |

The whole early game is the two steps of that ladder: a wooden pickaxe gets you
stone, a stone pickaxe gets you iron.

## Which one to carry

**A stone pickaxe, nearly always.** Cobblestone is free the moment you are
underground, 130 uses is a good stretch of tunnel, and it opens everything in the
game including iron ore. There is no reason to be careful with one.

The wooden pickaxe exists to get you your first eight cobblestone and should then go
in the furnace as fuel.

An iron pickaxe is *nice* — twice as fast as stone, twice the uses — but three ingots
is also a bucket, or most of a stove. Make one once iron has stopped being precious,
and make it before you go looking for titanium rather than after.

A titanium pickaxe is the fastest thing in the game and 400 uses is a long way. It is
worth one if you spend real time in the [Deep Mine](../world/deep-mine.html); it is
not worth it for the surface.

## As a weapon

A pickaxe does **1 + its tier** damage: 2 wooden, 3 stone, 4 iron, 5 titanium. That is
worse than an axe of the same tier. If you are planning to kill something, hold the axe.
