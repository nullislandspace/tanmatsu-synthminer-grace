| **Recipe** | 3 planks + 2 sticks | 3 cobblestone + 2 sticks | 3 iron ingots + 2 sticks |

| **Damage** | **3** | **4** | **5** |

Made at a [crafting table](../devices/crafting-table.html).

## What it speeds up

Everything wooden. Nothing *requires* an axe — you can pull a tree down bare-handed
— but wood is what you break most of, so an axe pays for itself immediately.

| Block | Hand | Wooden | Stone | Iron |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Log or planks | 2 s | 1 s | **0.5 s** | 0.3 s |
| Crafting table, chest, composter, cheese maker | 2.5 s | 1.3 s | 0.6 s | 0.4 s |
| Fence, gate, sign | 2 s | 1 s | 0.5 s | 0.3 s |
| Bed | 1 s | 0.5 s | 0.3 s | 0.2 s |

Remember that **one swing fells a whole tree**: a stone axe on a trunk is half a
second for thirty or forty logs. See [Trees and saplings](../plants/trees.html).

## It is the weapon

There is no sword in SynthMiner. The axe hits hardest because it is the heaviest
thing in the game:

| | Damage | Pig or cow (10 health) | Sheep or dog (8) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fist | 1 | 10 hits | 8 |
| Wooden axe | 3 | 4 | 3 |
| Stone axe | 4 | 3 | 2 |
| **Iron axe** | 5 | **2** | **2** |

Animals are briefly unhittable after each blow, so this is about two hits a second
at best.

## Which one to carry

A **stone axe** covers everything. The iron axe is worth it mainly for the damage —
two hits to kill a cow instead of three — and for clearing a lot of forest.

Wooden tools burn: a spent wooden axe is 200 ticks of furnace fuel.
