## What it actually costs

The rod itself is nearly free. What fishing costs is **bait**, and bait is
[**worms**](../devices/composter.html) — which come out of a composter, along with
the compost.

**One worm per cast, not per fish.** A cast that brings nothing up still cost a worm.
A cast that catches three fish in a row cost one.

The rod does not wear out because fishing is already paid for in worms, and charging
twice for one activity is how a system stops being worth using.

## What the string costs

Two string is the real price of the rod, and string comes from **wool**:

> 1 wool → 3 string · 3 sticks + 2 string → a rod

So a rod costs about **two thirds of one sheep's shearing**, and wool wants
[shears](shears.html), and shears want iron. Fishing is not something you do on your
first afternoon — which is right, because the composter that feeds it is not either.

See [Sheep](../animals/sheep.html).

## Using it

Point at water within reach and press <kbd>E</kbd>. **Two taps, not one:**

1. **Cast.** The float goes out and one worm is spent.
2. **Wait** — 4 to 14 seconds. The float dips and there is a splash when something
   bites.
3. **Strike** — press <kbd>E</kbd> again within about **1.5 seconds** of the bite.

Miss the window and you have lost nothing but the moment: the line stays out and
another fish comes along. Press <kbd>E</kbd> when nothing is biting and you reel in,
which *does* forfeit the worm — so if you are not sure, wait.

Full detail on [Fishing](../mechanics/fishing.html).

## What comes up

| Fish | Roughly |
|---|---|
| **Sardine** | half of catches |
| **Shrimp** | three in ten |
| **Salmon** | two in ten |

None of them is food raw. A [kitchen stove](../devices/kitchen-stove.html) turns
salmon into smoked salmon or sashimi, and shrimp into grilled shrimp — and **two
shrimp are one of the four things a pizza needs.**
