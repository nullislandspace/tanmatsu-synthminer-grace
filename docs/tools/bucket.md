---
title: Bucket
summary: Three iron ingots, never wears out, and does three completely different jobs.
---

| | |
|---|---|
| **Recipe** | **3 iron ingots**, at a [crafting table](../devices/crafting-table.html) |
| **Uses** | Unlimited — a bucket never wears out |
| **Stacks** | No. Neither empty nor full |

Probably the most useful single item in the game, and the first thing you should
spend iron on.

## Water

- **Fill it** by pressing <kbd>E</kbd> on a water **source**. Only a source: dipping
  it in flowing water gets you nothing, because flow is water going past rather than
  water that is there.
- **Empty it** by pressing <kbd>E</kbd> on a block face. A new source appears there.

<p class="note"><b>One bucket is infinite water.</b> Dig a two-block trench, put a
source in each end, and the cell between them becomes a source of its own. Fill from
it as often as you like, for ever. That is how you water a field a long way from any
lake — one trip with a bucket, and you never make another.</p>

This matters because [farming](../plants/index.html) is gated on water: tilled soil
within 4 blocks of water is wet and takes seeds, and dry soil refuses them.

## Milk

Press <kbd>E</kbd> on an adult [cow](../animals/cow.html) with an empty bucket and it
becomes a **bucket of milk**. No cooldown, no limit.

Milk is worth having for two reasons:

- **You can drink it** — one of only two foods in the game that needs no cooking
  (the other is a raw tomato). **The pail comes back empty** when you do.
- **It makes cheese**, in a [cheese maker](../devices/cheese-maker.html): 1 bucket
  of milk → 1 cheese, and **the bucket comes straight back** the moment the milk goes
  in, rather than being locked up for the day the cheese takes.

Cheese is 2 hunger and 2 saturation, and it is one of the four things a **pizza**
needs.

## Getting down a cliff

Water cancels fall damage completely, from any height. A bucket in your inventory is
an emergency exit: empty it at the bottom of the drop you are about to make, or into
the shaft you do not want to dig stairs down.

## Why it does not stack

Empty buckets do not stack and neither do full ones. If they did, filling one out of
a stack of sixteen would have to find a *second* slot for the full one — which might
not exist, and then the water would have to go somewhere. At one apiece the stack is
simply swapped in place and there is nothing to get wrong.

Iron is cheap enough by the time this matters. Carry two.
