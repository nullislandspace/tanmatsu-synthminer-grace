**Fred can feed himself now.** Farming, animals, cooking and fishing — plus water that
flows and that you can see through.

Worlds from v0.1.0 open unchanged. 32 languages.

## Farming

A whole second act to the game. [More →](../plants/index.html)

- The **hoe**, in three tiers, which turns grass into farmland.
- **Wet and dry soil.** Tilled ground within 4 blocks of water is wet and takes seeds; dry
  ground refuses them and says so.
- **Five crops** — wheat, potatoes, tomatoes, beans and rice — each with four growth stages,
  each with its own timing, and each found growing wild in its own biome. Rice is planted *in*
  shallow water and stands two blocks tall.
- **Wheat seeds** come out of tall grass, so wheat is available on your first day.
- The **composter**: leaves, flowers, grass and crop waste become **compost** (which pushes a
  crop on a whole stage) and **worms** (which are fishing bait).
- Crops keep growing while you are away, and catch up the moment you come back.

## Animals

Four kinds, placed with the land rather than spawned at you. [More →](../animals/index.html)

- **Pigs** (pork), **cows** (beef and **milk**), **sheep** (mutton and **wool**) and **dogs**.
- **Feeding and breeding.** Two animals fed their own food will walk to each other and produce
  a calf; a calf follows whoever holds its food and grows up in ten minutes.
- **Shears** take wool off a sheep without killing it, and the fleece grows back — the only
  thing in the game an animal gives more than once.
- A **bucket** on a cow gives milk, as often as you like.
- **Dogs are tamed with a bone**, and a bone comes out of a sausage maker.
- **Fences and gates** to keep a herd where you put it.
- **A bed** — three wool and three planks. It skips the night *and* sets where you respawn.
- The **cheese maker** (milk → cheese, a day) and the **sausage maker** (pork + a flower →
  sausage in a minute, and sometimes a bone).

## Food, hunger and the fall

[More →](../mechanics/hunger.html)

- **Hunger and saturation.** Ten drumsticks, plus an invisible reserve that is spent first —
  which is why a good meal lasts longer than its drumsticks say. Walking, swimming, jumping,
  mining and healing all cost.
- **Health comes back by itself** while you are well fed, and healing is expensive. **Starving
  kills.**
- The **kitchen stove** — and it cooks out of **the chest standing next to it**, because a
  pizza wants four different things and filling four slots by hand is a chore. One item puts
  down both blocks. It tells you which ingredient is missing.
- **Thirteen dishes**, from a baked potato to a pizza. [The table →](../food.html)
- **Fall damage.** Three blocks free, half a heart a block after that, measured by height so a
  ledge breaks a fall — and water cancels the lot.
- **Death and respawn**: half your health, your whole inventory, at your bed.

## Fishing

[More →](../mechanics/fishing.html)

- A **rod** of 3 sticks and 2 string. It never wears out.
- **Worms are the bait** — one per cast, not per fish — which is what the composter is for.
- **Two taps**: cast, wait 4 to 14 seconds, and strike within a second and a half of the bite.
  A missed bite costs nothing; the line stays out.
- Sardines, salmon and shrimp. Salmon and shrimp go into the best dishes in the game.

## Water

[More →](../world/water.html)

- **Water flows.** Sources spread sideways, thin as they go, and fall as waterfalls.
- **Two sources make a third**, so a two-block trench and a bucket is an infinite spring.
- **Flowing water is visibly shallower** than a source, and a falling column reads as a
  waterfall.
- **Water is transparent by default** — you can see into a lake, which is how you find the sand
  rice grows on.

## Renewable wood and flowers

[More →](../plants/trees.html)

- Felling a tree drops **one or two saplings** of the species you cut. Plant one on grass or
  dirt and it grows into a tree in a day.
- **Compost thrown on a grass block grows a 3×3 patch of yellow flowers**, which is what keeps
  a sausage farm supplied.

Between them these close the last hole in the economy: before this, wood and flowers were the
only two things that could be used up and never got back.

## Fixed

- **Blocks that did not appear at all**, and leaves that felled whole trees when broken.
- A **cactus** now comes down as one column, like a tree.
- The **main menu took fifteen seconds** to open. It does not now.
- **SD card write timeouts** — they were the radio, not the card.
- The **bottom of the world** could be dug through.
- The **sausage maker was a plain white block** — its texture was over-bright and had been
  since it shipped. `make texcheck` now opens every texture in the game and fails on this class
  of mistake.
- The sausage maker would not say **what it was waiting for**; now it names the missing
  ingredient, hands back ingredients it cannot use, and every slot has a "take it out" row.
- **Animals stopped appearing** after enough walking — the live-creature pool filled up and
  quietly never spawned anything again. Wild dogs had never been seen by anyone.
- A **flight recorder**, so a crash on the badge leaves a trace behind it.
- Faster region file handling, and a much cheaper card.
