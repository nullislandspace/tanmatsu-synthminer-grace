---
title: "v0.2.1 — What Playing It Found"
summary: Released 1 October 2026. Six bugs that only a long session could show.
---

A fixes release, and every one of these came out of one long play session on
v0.2.0 plus the flight recorder it left behind. No new content.

Worlds from v0.1.0 and v0.2.0 open unchanged.

## Breeding works again

**Animals stopped breeding after you had walked far enough, and a fresh load fixed
it until you walked far enough again.**

A chunk leaving the loaded world never released its animals. They stayed in the
live-creature pool as orphans, and the pool is 128: on the save this was found on,
it **filled after 94 chunks** of walking. After that nothing could be born at all —
feeding two animals did everything it was supposed to except produce a calf.

The same hole explains the rest of it:

- **Animals appearing merged into each other**, facing opposite ways — coming back
  to a field loaded its animals *again* on top of the orphans, so a place visited
  five times held five copies of every cow.
- **Animals standing inside you** — an orphan is not in a loaded chunk, so the rule
  that pushes bodies apart skipped it entirely.
- **Chests and furnaces** leaked the same way, 192 slots of them.

## Felling takes one tree

In a thick forest, one swing of an axe could walk from the canopy you hit into its
neighbours and keep going: **108 to 494 blocks and 9 to 36 separate trunks** per
swing, measured.

It also broke what felling is for — a fell drops one or two saplings *per swing*, so
clearing a grove of thirty trees handed back two seedlings and wood stopped being
renewable in the one biome where most of it is cut.

A fell now follows the trunk you hit and clears the canopy around its top. **A
neighbour whose branches are touching yours keeps every log.** 54 to 72 blocks, one
tree, every time.

## Blocks you broke stay broken

**Trees could go on standing after you cut them down**, and anything else you changed
could stay on screen — for up to a minute or two, with no way to force it.

The mesh cache was unbounded. The near view draws to 40 blocks but keeps chunks
loaded out to 80, so most of what it held was geometry that would never be drawn
again, and nothing ever freed it. On the session this was found in it reached **10.2
MB with 70 KiB of memory left** — and once memory is gone, rebuilding a piece of the
world *fails*, so the old picture stays. It is now bounded, every frame.

## Animals steer properly

- **An animal in water no longer spins on the spot.** A swimmer bobs, and on the tick
  its feet cleared the surface it decided the water in front of it was a hazard and
  turned away — then sank, swam on, bobbed, and turned again. Measured at **1.9
  reversals a second**; now 0.1. With no shore in reach it holds one heading and
  swims, which gets it out of a lake.
- **An animal following you stops two blocks short** instead of walking into you.
- **It notices you from 12 blocks**, up from 7 — seven was inside the fog, so leading
  a herd meant walking into each animal in turn.
- Nothing turns through a big angle more than once a second.

## Things you can now see

- **Wild potatoes, beans and tomatoes** were the same colour as the tall grass they
  grow in — a ripe potato was 21 units from it, where ripe wheat is 145. Ripe crops
  now take the colour of their harvest, as wheat already did, and carry a proper
  cluster of fruit. Potato 21 → 103, beans 35 → 93, tomato 32 → 61.
- **A fence gate** is a visibly darker timber than the fence it stands in, so the one
  block in a run you can walk through looks like it.

## Other fixes

- **Leaves now drop**, to [shears](../tools/shears.html) only. The composter has
  listed leaves as compostable since farming shipped and there was no way to hold
  one — a canopy is the biggest pile of green matter in the game.
- **Gates and machines no longer refuse a click** because of what you are holding.
  Walking up to a gate with wheat in hand told you to go and find some tilled soil
  and left the gate shut.

## For the curious

The flight recorder earned its keep twice over: the stale-geometry bug was found by
correlating 182 edits against free memory in a 27-minute trace, and the felling bug
was confirmed by replaying the session's own seven fells against the same world seed
on a PC. Five new host checks guard these so they cannot come back quietly.
