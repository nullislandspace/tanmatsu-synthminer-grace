**Nothing in your hand hurts a living thing any more — except a sword.** An axe, a
pickaxe, a shovel, a hoe, a fist: all of them do **zero** damage to a creature. Meat and
self-defence now both run through a **smithing forge** built out of twelve diamonds —
which is why the [Deep Mine](../world/deep-mine.html) got *more* dangerous in the same
release rather than safer.

Worlds from v0.1.0 through v0.3.0 open unchanged. 32 languages.

## The axe is not a weapon

[More →](../tools/sword.html)

**Only a weapon can damage a creature.** Swing at a cow with a titanium axe and the swing
will not land: the HUD says **"A tool will not hurt it"** and the sound says the same.

- <kbd>Q</kbd> still swings at anything — what changed is what can land a blow.
- The axe is back to being the thing that fells trees, which is what it always was.
- A sword digs nothing, so mining with one is the expensive way to lose a weapon.

That moves the last thing the game used to give you for free — killing an animal for meat
— behind the forge, and the forge behind [12 diamonds](../ores/diamond.html), which only
exist downstairs.

## The smithing forge

[More →](../devices/smithing-forge.html)

**12 diamonds + 10 planks at a crafting table**, and it places **two blocks that know each
other**: the forge and the chest it takes its metal from, the same trick the kitchen stove
uses. It looks like a furnace cut out of blue diamond.

- A **recipe selector** and an explicit **"Start crafting"** that makes **exactly one
  item** and stops. It is the only machine in the game with a start and a stop.
- **The ingots are spent the moment you press Start** and live in the forge for the rest of
  the job. A forge whose chest was emptied, robbed or buried still finishes.
- **An iron item takes one in-game day** — 20 playing minutes — **a titanium one two**.
- **15 coal** covers an iron job, **30** a titanium one, fed as the fire goes down.
- **Out of coal mid-job it stops where it is and waits**, keeping its progress rather than
  starting over. Every other machine in the game throws the part-cooked item away.
- **While a job runs the selector is locked**, because it names the metal that was already
  paid for. The fuel slot stays open.
- **Flames come out of the top while it works**, and stop when it is finished or starved.

A sword and a full set of armour is **20 ingots and ten jobs**.

**Iron first.** Two ingots and a day, and everything on the surface dies in one or two
swings. **Titanium is the Deep Mine sword** — make it before you start claiming ground
underground rather than after something has killed you once.

## Armour, and why the mobs hit harder now

[More →](../tools/armour.html)

Four pieces — helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots — in iron or titanium. **The guard is a
percentage of the blow, not a number subtracted from it**, and titanium is exactly twice
iron piece for piece:

| | Helmet | Chestplate | Leggings | Boots | **Full set** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Iron | 9% | 16% | 12% | 8% | **45%** |
| Titanium | 18% | 32% | 24% | 16% | **90%** |

**The mobs were retuned in the same breath**, because a guard is only worth wearing if
there is something to guard against. A zombie used to hit for 3; it now hits for **10**, a
skeleton for **8**:

| | Unarmoured | Iron set | Titanium set |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zombie, 10 a swing | 2 swings to die | 4 swings | **20 swings — half a heart each** |
| Skeleton, 8 a swing | 3 swings to die | 5 swings | **20 swings — half a heart each** |

**Something always lands**: a blow is `max(1, attack * (100 - guard) / 100)`, rounded down,
so no mob number becomes useless at a higher tier. 150 uses a piece in iron, 300 in
titanium, and **every blow you take wears one use off every piece you are wearing**.

- **Putting it on is a player figure in the Tab screen**: select a piece on the figure and
  press Enter to take it off, select one in the grid and press Enter to put it on. A worn
  piece is not one of the 24 inventory slots.
- **It shows on you** in third person, piece by piece — an iron helmet on a titanium
  chestplate is exactly what you asked for.
- **A swing is animated and it makes a noise**: a whoosh for a swing that finds nothing, a
  clang for one that lands.
- **Armour guards a blow, not the cave.** It does nothing against lava — 2 health every
  half second — and nothing against a fall. On purpose.

## Titanium

[More →](../ores/titanium.html)

The Deep Mine's second gem-grade ore, and the first block in the game that **needs an iron
pickaxe** — too weak a tool still breaks it and drops nothing.

- **As rare as diamond, literally the same roll**: 0.05% of the cave's rock against
  diamond's 0.06%, over 144 measured chunks. Iron is 44× either of them.
- **Smelted into ingots in an ordinary furnace**, then made at tier 4: pickaxe, axe and
  shovel at iron's prices, **400 uses**, and twice iron's speed — 1.5 s on a block of
  titanium ore against iron's 2.0 and bare hands' 12.0.
- No titanium hoe: three tools were asked for, and a hoe does not dig.

## Machines you can see working

[More →](../mechanics/time.html)

A machine is now **alive while its chunk is resident**: it advances every tick, and a chunk
that comes back gets its whole absence in one go. What that buys you is telling what needs
attention from across a room.

- **Fire above a burning furnace, kitchen stove or smithing forge** — and no fire when the
  output is full or the fuel has run out, so a dark forge reads as *out of fuel* and burns
  no more coal.
- **A haze above a composter** with something ready in it.
- **The sausage maker's side plate goes dark red** when its output is not empty.
- **The cheese maker's barrel turns yellow when it is done**, without anybody opening it.

## Fixed

- **Ripe rice left a hole in the pond.** The water surface was drawn only where the cell
  above the water was air, and a two-block-tall rice plant is not air — so every paddy that
  had grown up was a plant-shaped hole at exactly the plane the pond should close at.
- **A machine could start half finished.** The cheese maker — and the composter and sausage
  maker with it — banked an absence shorter than one unit, because the rule that says *an
  idle machine does not accumulate* lived inside the loop it was supposed to guard. That is
  the "why did this start at 70%".
- **The cheese maker's barrel was only correct after you opened it.** The interface was the
  only thing writing that colour.

## Under the hood

- **Three new columns on the item table**, appended last: `attack`, `guard` (a percentage)
  and `wear_slot`, so a piece of armour names the place on the body it belongs to instead of
  having it inferred from where its id happens to sit.
- **A forge job is a new 32-bit field on the block record**, saved under its own name,
  because the 16-bit one every other machine uses stops at 32767 and a titanium job is 48000
  ticks.
- **The 3D engine moved to synthengine3D V2.3**, which brings the emissive-triangle merge
  that the forge flame and the torch both use.
- **`tolower` / `toupper` and the SoC temperature sensor** came across from graceloader.
